# GTA V Takeover

**Portal 2** × GTA V · visual · idea by the team

Bring GTA V into the game in the most visible and funny way. The change shows in the first 30 seconds of play.

> I built GTA V in Portal 2: real 3D taxis and police cars, a minimap, wanted stars, WASTED slow-mo and cash rain.

## Install

Copy the mod folder's scripts, sound, materials and models folders into a Portal 2 mod folder (for example portal2/sigf), then launch Portal 2 with -game sigf and load sp_a2_triple_laser. The chase starts by itself.
